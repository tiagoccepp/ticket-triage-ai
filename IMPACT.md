# Why this matters to an organization

## Who feels the pain

Support queues fill faster than humans can triage. Naive auto-routing
misroutes tickets and erodes trust; pure-manual triage burns reviewer hours
on obvious cases while urgent ones wait.

## What changes with this system

- **Automate the confident, queue the uncertain.** High-confidence tickets
  route instantly; low-confidence, high-severity, disagreement and abstention
  cases land in a review queue with the model's reasoning attached.
- **Taxonomy as data.** Adding a category is a config edit, not a retrain —
  the triage process adapts at the speed of the business.
- **The metric that builds trust.** Accuracy-on-routed (not raw accuracy)
  tells leadership exactly how safe the automation is; the review rate tells
  them what it costs in human time.

## Metrics a team would track

- Median time-to-first-route; misroute rate on auto-routed tickets.
- Review rate + reviewer throughput (decisions/hour with vs without assistance).
- Accuracy-on-routed and disagreement rate across taxonomy/model changes.
